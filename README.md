# 🌱 FarmGeni

### Smart Farm Management & Agricultural Decision Support Platform

FarmGeni is a web-based smart farm management platform designed to help farmers
manage crops, track farming activities, monitor expenses and income, analyze
profitability, and access useful agricultural insights from a centralized
dashboard.

The goal of FarmGeni is to simplify farm management by bringing important
farming and financial information together in one easy-to-use platform.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🌾 Farm Dashboard
- Overview of farm activities
- Total crops and farming records
- Income and expense summary
- Profit and loss overview
- Recent activities
- Upcoming tasks

### 🌱 Crop Management
- Add and manage crops
- Track crop information
- Monitor crop activities
- Manage crop-related records
- Track crop progress

### 💰 Expense Management
- Record farming expenses
- Categorize expenses
- Track spending
- Monitor overall farm investment

### 💵 Income Management
- Record crop and farm income
- Track revenue
- Compare income with expenses
- Calculate overall profitability

### 📊 Analytics
- Visual representation of farm data
- Income analysis
- Expense analysis
- Profit and loss insights
- Farming performance overview

### 🌦️ Weather Information
- Weather information for farming activities
- Temperature and environmental information
- Weather-based farming awareness

### 🧠 Farm Intelligence
- Agricultural insights
- Crop-related recommendations
- Farming activity support
- Data-driven decision assistance

### 📋 Tasks & Activities
- Create farming tasks
- Track pending activities
- Manage completed activities
- Organize farming operations

### 🐄 Livestock Management
- Maintain livestock-related information
- Track livestock records
- Organize farm resources

### 📦 Inventory Management
- Manage farm resources and inventory
- Track available items
- Monitor farm-related stock

### 📄 Reports
- View farm-related reports
- Analyze financial information
- Generate useful farm records
